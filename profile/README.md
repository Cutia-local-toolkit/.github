## Closing Notes for Cutia

Treat Cutia as a habit, not as a one-time cleanup tool. Open Cutia when you start work. Keep Today small. Move finished items out. Let Archive stay quiet. That rhythm is what makes Cutia useful over months, not only on the first day.

## Why Cutia Exists

Many people jump between browser tabs, scattered folders, and separate utilities just to finish one simple task. Cutia exists to reduce that noise. Cutia gathers the core actions you need into a single desktop flow, so you spend less time searching and more time finishing work. Cutia is built for users who want clarity, not complexity. If your day is filled with small repetitive steps, Cutia gives those steps a stable home on Windows.

Cutia solves one practical problem: too many tools for one routine. With Cutia, you open one workspace, keep your recent items nearby, and continue without resetting your setup every morning.

Cutia is especially helpful when your week includes mixed tasks: study notes in the morning, client files in the afternoon, and personal documents in the evening. Instead of rebuilding your layout each time, Cutia keeps one familiar structure ready.

## Quick Start with Cutia

1. **Get Cutia** — Grab the official installer from the button above (Windows 10/11 only).
2. **Install** — Run `Cutia_Setup.exe`. The app opens in under 3 seconds after installation.
3. **Create your profile** — Start with a local profile for everyday use. No phone number required.
4. **Pin your folders** — Choose active project folders and pin them inside Cutia.
5. **Set Today items** — Add a short list of files and routines for the current day.
6. **Keep Archive quiet** — Move finished items out of Today so the workspace stays calm.
7. **Work normally** — Cutia stays beside your editor and browser while you finish tasks.
